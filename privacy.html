YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This is a personal IT portfolio. There are no visitor accounts, advertising tools, analytics dashboards, mailing-list sign-ups or contact forms on this website.

Who is responsible

I am Ioannis Tsakalidis, based in Larissa, Greece, the owner of tsakioan.com and the controller of personal information I handle through portfolio enquiries. For privacy questions or requests, contact info@tsakioan.com.

When you visit the website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, including your IP address, which GitHub logs for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Fonts, scripts and the downloadable test sheets are served from this website. Pages do not load content from Google Fonts, analytics providers or other third-party servers.

Cookies and language preference

The website’s own code does not set cookies or include analytics or advertising trackers. The website remembers whether you last viewed the English or Greek version, in your browser’s local storage under tsakioan-language, and uses it to open the matching version on later visits. This preference is not sent to me and remains until you clear the site’s browser data or change it. The interactive circuit board does not submit information.

When you email me

If you contact me, I receive your email address, any name you provide, the message, attachments and normal email metadata. I use this information to answer your enquiry and manage related professional correspondence. Providing it is voluntary, but I need a way to contact you to reply.

For general enquiries, the legal basis is my legitimate interest in responding to people who contact me and maintaining relevant professional communications. Where you request steps towards a contract with me, processing necessary for those steps is based on that request. Information may also be retained where required by law.

I do not operate a marketing mailing list or buy contact databases. Contacting me does not subscribe you to newsletters, and I do not sell your personal information. I do not use it for profiling or automated decisions.

Services that handle email

Mail sent to info@tsakioan.com is forwarded by Porkbun to my Gmail inbox. These providers process email content and delivery information to forward, store and secure messages.

Details are available in the privacy notices for Porkbun and Google.

These services may process information outside Greece and the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Their linked privacy and data-processing documents explain their processing locations and applicable transfer safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses or adequacy arrangements. You can contact me for information about the services used for your correspondence.

How long information is kept

I keep correspondence for as long as needed to answer the enquiry and manage the resulting conversation or professional relationship. The relevant criteria are whether the matter is still active, whether records are needed for follow-up, and any applicable legal or dispute-related retention needs. Messages no longer needed for those purposes should be deleted. Service providers manage their own security logs and backup retention under their policies.

Your choices and rights

Where applicable under data-protection law, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your information, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Email info@tsakioan.com to make a request. I may need proportionate information to verify that the request relates to you.

You can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority or your local data-protection authority.

External links and updates

Links to LinkedIn and other services take you to websites governed by their own privacy notices. This policy will be updated when the site or its handling of information changes; the date above identifies the latest revision.
